🦍 MONKE ARENA

A Gorilla Tag-style arm-locomotion playground for WebXR

How to move (like a monke)

  • Push surfaces with your hands. Pushing a hand into a surface launches you the opposite way — paddle the floor to run, slap down to jump, shove a wall to ricochet.
  • Touch a surface to catch. A still hand stops you dead, so you can hang, climb and steer on anything.
  • Fly into a wall and you bounce. Harder pushes = bigger launches.
  • Right thumbstick left/right = snap turn.
  • Collect all 14 bananas — touch them with a hand or your body. They respawn when you clear the set.

Desktop preview (no headset)

  • Click the view to capture the mouse · WASD fly · Space/C up/down · Shift fast · Esc release mouse.

Fan-made tech demo — not affiliated with Another Axiom. For VR you need a WebXR browser (e.g. Quest) served over HTTPS or localhost.
